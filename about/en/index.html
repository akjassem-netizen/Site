About Us - Law Office of Ali Kadhem Hashimi
The Law Office of Ali Kadhem Hashimi is an advanced professional legal institution in Baghdad, providing comprehensive legal and advisory services to individuals, local companies, investors, and foreign corporations in Iraq. We bear the responsibility of delivering the highest standards of precision and professionalism to protect our clients' rights and interests effectively.

The office is characterized by a clear strategic vision based on combining depth in interpreting active Iraqi legislation with speed and innovation in finding legal and alternative solutions. Our professional journey relies on core pillars including:
  • Corporate Expertise & Formation: A proven track record in foreign and local company formation, drafting major contracts, and resolving complex disputes.
  • Compliance & Due Diligence Lawyer: Providing background checks, financial vetting, and compliance auditing for corporations and investors.
  • Tax Residency & Investment Affairs: Managing residency permits, tax matters, and protecting investments under Iraqi laws.
  • Reliable Judicial Representation: Handling legal advocacy across all courts and judicial authorities in Iraq with high efficiency.
The office always strives to be the trusted legal partner for anyone seeking legal security and institutional success in the Iraqi market.
