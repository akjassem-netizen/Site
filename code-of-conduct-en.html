Code of Conduct & Professional Ethics

AKH Law Office believes that delivering elite legal services and establishing commercial entities are anchored upon unwavering pillars of integrity and professionalism. Operating under the laws of the Republic of Iraq, the Firm adheres to professional conduct rules issued in accordance with the Iraqi Bar Association Law No. (173) of 1965 (as amended), aligning with best international practices and standards to serve all local and international corporate clients, investors, and individuals.

1. Absolute Confidentiality & Institutional Data Privacy

All client communications, legal documents, and commercial data are subject to absolute and sacred confidentiality under professional standards. The Firm implements rigorous digital security measures (aligned with international information security frameworks), securing client and corporate documents within protected cloud environments, ensuring no disclosure to any third party except through explicit consent or mandatory judicial orders authorized by law.

2. Strict Conflict of Interest Management & Impartiality

The Firm enforces a stringent regulatory system to verify that any new retainer or consultation is entirely free from conflicts with the interests of our current or former clients. The Firm categorically declines to represent opposing parties in the same dispute, safeguarding the independence of legal decisions and absolute impartiality for all clients.

3. Institutional Integrity & Official Dealings

The Firm maintains the highest standards of transparency and integrity when representing clients and executing transactions before judicial authorities across all jurisdictions, as well as official, semi-official, and governmental bodies. We adhere strictly to formal legal pathways in compliance with international anti-corruption standards.

4. Standard of Professional Care & Legal Realism

The Firm is committed to exercising customary professional care and diligent legal efforts consistent with professional honor and principles, while delivering objective and accurate advisory. Acknowledging the nature of judicial and regulatory authorities, the Firm does not issue absolute promises or guarantees regarding the outcomes of cases or administrative decisions, but rather provides an independent, professional legal assessment that protects the client's interests.

5. Operational Limits & Regulatory Dependencies

Timeframes for completing transactions before official authorities and governmental directorates are subject to administrative and regulatory constraints inherent to those entities, falling beyond the direct control of the Firm. Successful execution is conditional upon the client's commitment to providing accurate legal documents and data within specified deadlines. Accordingly, the Firm assumes no liability for delays resulting from missing client documents or bureaucratic processes of official authorities, while remaining fully dedicated to diligent follow-up and prompt transparency.

6. Comprehensive Corporate & Individual Representation

The Firm provides comprehensive legal care for individuals, local enterprises, foreign corporations, and international investors, encompassing corporate formation, commercial contract drafting, and representation before all official and judicial authorities, guiding clients precisely through Iraqi regulations to ensure complete legal and investment security.
