Professional Code of Conduct & Business Ethics

AKH Law Office believes that providing legal services and establishing commercial entities relies on firm pillars of integrity and professionalism. The office operates under the laws in force in the Republic of Iraq, based on the rules of professional conduct issued pursuant to the provisions of the Iraqi Advocacy Law No. (173) of 1965 (as amended), and in compliance with best international practices and standards to serve all clients from local and foreign companies, investors, and individuals.

1. Absolute Confidentiality & Data Privacy السرية المطلقة وحماية البيانات المؤسسية

All correspondence, documents, and commercial data of clients are subject to absolute and sacred confidentiality in accordance with professional rules. The office adheres to strict digital security standards (in line with global information security standards), ensuring client and corporate documents are stored in secure cloud environments, and no information is disclosed to any third party except with explicit consent or pursuant to a mandatory judicial order permitted by law.

2. Strict Conflict of Interest Management حوكمة تضارب المصالح والحياد التام

The office applies a strict supervisory system to verify that any new mandate or consultation is free from any conflict with the interests of our current or former clients. The office strictly refuses to represent opposing parties in the same dispute, ensuring the independence of legal decisions and absolute neutrality for all clients.

3. Institutional Integrity & Official Dealings النزاهة المؤسسية والتعاملات الرسمية

The office adopts the highest standards of transparency and integrity in representing clients and processing transactions before judicial authorities of various types and jurisdictions, as well as official and semi-official departments and other government entities. We strictly adhere to official legal pathways and comply with international anti-corruption standards.

4. Standard of Professional Care & Legal Realism معيار العناية المهنية المعتادة والواقعية القانونية

The office commits to exercising standard professional care and diligent legal effort consistent with the honor and principles of the profession, providing objective and accurate consultations. Recognizing the nature of judicial and regulatory authorities, the office does not make promises or definitive guarantees regarding the outcomes of cases or administrative decisions, but rather provides an independent, professional legal assessment that protects the client's interests.

5. Operational Limits & Regulatory Dependencies الحدود التشغيلية والاعتماد على الوثائق الرسمية

Timelines for completing transactions before official authorities and government departments are subject to the administrative and regulatory rules of those entities, which are beyond the direct control of the office. To complete any task, the client is required to provide all correct legal documents and data within specified deadlines; accordingly, the office bears no responsibility for delays resulting from missing documents on the part of the client or from the administrative bureaucracy of official authorities, while we remain fully committed to diligent follow-up and immediate transparency.

6. Comprehensive Corporate & Client Representation التمثيل الشامل للشركات والأفراد

The office provides comprehensive legal care for all individuals, local and foreign companies, and international investors; including company registration, drafting commercial contracts, and representation before all official and judicial authorities, while fully acquainting clients with Iraqi legislative and regulatory frameworks to ensure complete legal and investment security.
