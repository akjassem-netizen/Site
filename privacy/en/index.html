Privacy Policy

The Law Office of Ali Kadhem Hashimi places great importance on the confidentiality and privacy of our visitors' and clients' data. This Privacy Policy document outlines how information is collected, used, and protected when you visit our website.

1. Data Collection and Usage (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website, helping us improve the browsing experience and develop our legal services. This tool automatically collects anonymous data using cookies, such as device type (laptop or smartphone), browser used, and pages visited.

To learn more about how Google processes data and how to opt-out, you can visit Google's official privacy policy page at:
https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/partners/

2. Professional Confidentiality & Client Data Protection

In compliance with legal ethics and applicable laws in the Republic of Iraq, all correspondence, legal consultations, or information provided by clients through official communication channels and bookings are subject to absolute professional confidentiality and are never shared with any third party.

3. Cookies

The website uses cookies to enhance browsing performance and user preferences. You can control or disable cookie settings directly through your internet browser at any time.

4. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy from time to time in compliance with legal and technical developments, and any updates will be posted on this page.
